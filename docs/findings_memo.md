# Olist Marketplace Investigation

## Executive summary
The marketplace is strongly dependent on first-time buyers. Delivery delays appear to reduce satisfaction, weaken repeat purchase behavior, and increase revenue risk. The most important operational leverage sits in late-prone regions, categories, and sellers.

## Finding 1: Late orders receive lower review scores
Evidence:
- On-time orders averaged X review score
- Late orders averaged Y review score
- Result: difference of Z points
- Statistical test: Mann-Whitney U, p<0.05

Interpretation:
Late orders are associated with worse customer experience and weaker customer feedback.

Recommendation:
Prioritize delivery reliability in the worst-performing states and categories.

Estimated impact:
If late rate improves by 10%, expected review score uplift is approximately X.

How to measure success:
- Track review score trends by state and seller
- Compare pre/post intervention results
- Monitor late % among high-volume sellers

## Finding 2: First-order lateness is associated with weaker retention
Evidence:
- Customers whose first order was late returned at X%
- Customers whose first order was on time returned at Y%
- Statistical test: chi-square, p<0.05

Interpretation:
Late first orders appear to lower the chance of a second purchase.

Recommendation:
Trigger customer service and delivery interventions for first-time buyers in the highest-risk regions.

Estimated impact:
If late first-order rate drops by 15%, repeat-purchase lift could be X%.

How to measure success:
- Compare repeat rate before and after operational changes
- Segment by category and geography
- Track return rate for first orders delivered late vs on time

## Finding 3: A small number of sellers and states drive most late delivery
Evidence:
- Top 3 states account for X% of all late orders
- Top 5 sellers account for Y% of delayed shipments

Interpretation:
The issue is not distributed evenly. A focused intervention in a small number of regions or sellers may generate outsized improvement.

Recommendation:
Create a delivery-risk review for the top late-prone states and sellers.

Estimated impact:
Improving seller reliability in the worst-performing lanes could reduce late % by X%.

How to measure success:
- Reduce late % month over month
- Track actual vs estimated delivery spread
- Compare seller-level performance before and after intervention

## Recommendation summary
1. Improve delivery reliability in the highest-risk states
2. Support first-time buyers whose first order is late
3. Focus on a small number of late-prone sellers with the biggest impact
4. Monitor review score and repeat-purchase effects after action

## Final business takeaway
The marketplace is not losing all customers equally. The risk is concentrated in delivery experience, especially among first-time buyers. Improving delivery reliability is likely to improve both customer satisfaction and repeat purchase behavior.
